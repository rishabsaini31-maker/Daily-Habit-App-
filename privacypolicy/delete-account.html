How to Request Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with the Google Play Store User Data Policy, users of DHI (Daily Habit Improvement) can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the DHI app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile Avatar in the top-left corner of the Home screen to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the LEGAL & STORE COMPLIANCE section.
  4. Tap Permanently Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the action in the prompt. Your account, profile, streaks, and focus schedules will be permanently wiped from our cloud servers immediately (0 days retention).

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to submit a manual deletion request:

  1. Send an email to our support team at support@dhi.app.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - DHI.
  3. Include the email address associated with your DHI account.
  4. Our team will process the deletion and permanently erase all your data within 48 hours, sending you an email confirmation.
