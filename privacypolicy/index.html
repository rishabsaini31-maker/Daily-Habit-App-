3. Sensitive Android Permissions & API Disclosures

To provide surgical app blocking, screen-time regulation, and hardware sensor features, DHI requests specific Android system permissions:

A. Accessibility Service API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Prominent Disclosure on Accessibility Service Use:
DHI utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API strictly to detect when distracting social media feeds (specifically short-form video feeds like Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) or user-configured blocked applications are brought to the foreground during an active Focus Session or Smart Routine.

  • Strict Limitation: The Accessibility Service is used solely to identify the package name and active view identifiers of blocked apps in order to display the focus shield overlay.
  • No Personal Snooping: DHI never reads, stores, or transmits your keystrokes, passwords, private chat messages, web browsing history, financial accounts, or photos.
  • User Control: You must explicitly grant this permission in Android System Settings, and you can revoke it at any time.

B. Usage Stats Permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Allows DHI to query Android's UsageStatsManager to calculate cumulative foreground screen time and alert you when you have exceeded your configured daily app limits.

C. Physical Activity Recognition (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION)

Queries device step counter sensors (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) to track your daily steps in real-time, verifying whether you have met your daily walking goal to unlock screen time.

D. Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Enables DHI to display the dark focus shield overlay directly over an intercepted distracting app, reminding you to stay on track.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the DHI mobile app, Supabase cloud database, and backend APIs are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases utilize AES-256 encryption at rest to prevent unauthorized access.
  • In-Memory Processing: Focus session timers and real-time app interception decisions are evaluated instantaneously in device memory with 0ms latency.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

DHI engages trusted, enterprise-grade cloud providers to operate the platform:

  • Google Play Services / Billing: For distributing the app, license verification, and in-app subscription processing (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase: For cloud account authentication, row-level security (RLS), and database synchronization (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • RevenueCat: For managing and restoring mobile subscription entitlements across devices (RevenueCat Privacy Policy).

We do not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or data broker SDKs into DHI.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

DHI is designed for students, working professionals, and adults seeking digital wellness. The app is intended for individuals aged 13 years and older (or 16 in the European Union).

We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will promptly delete such information from our servers.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion Mechanism

In full compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store guidelines, you have complete control over your data:

  • Access & Export: You may view your profile, focus history, and synced schedules inside the app at any time.
  • In-App Permanent Deletion: You can permanently wipe your account and all associated cloud data by going to Settings -> Danger Zone -> Permanently Delete Account. This instantaneously purges your profile, streak records, gems, and routines from our servers.
  • Web-Based Data Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app and wish to have your data deleted, email us at support@dhi.app with your account email address, and our team will process the deletion within 48 hours.
